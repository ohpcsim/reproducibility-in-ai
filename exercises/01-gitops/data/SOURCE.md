# About this data

`co2_mm_mlo.csv` is the monthly mean CO2 record from Mauna Loa, Hawaii, as published by NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory. It is unchanged, with NOAA's header kept at the top of the file.

- Source: <https://gml.noaa.gov/webdata/ccgg/trends/co2/co2_mm_mlo.csv>
- Downloaded: November 7, 2026. NOAA's file is dated October 9, 2026.
- Terms: NOAA makes these data "freely available to the public and the scientific community" and asks for citation. The full text is in the file's header.
- Please cite: Dr. Xin Lan, NOAA/GML (gml.noaa.gov/ccgg/trends/) and Dr. Ralph Keeling, Scripps Institution of Oceanography (scrippsco2.ucsd.edu/).
